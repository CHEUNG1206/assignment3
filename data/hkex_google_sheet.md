# HKEX original market figures

Google Sheet: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Nr4XOrgmN3wJ44HHLVwDYQaRkt7oi6D_qz0EIGy9lJ0/edit

Read on 2026-10-07. Values were copied from HKEX PDFs, the HKEX Monthly Market Highlights page, and the HKEX New Listing Reports in `data/hkex_nlr/`. The Notes tab records which rows must not be added together.

- `Policy_funding`: every funding sentence, its verb as printed, and whether the money is new in that address. Only new money counts towards an address's total.
- `Year_after`: new and already-in-place funding beside the board IPO and turnover in the next calendar year. Funding is not divided by IPO funds. 2027 is not published.
- `Industry`: each policy industry's Main Board IPOs from each delivery date to the day before the next address, with the share of the Main Board in the same window, and the companies the name screen misses.
- `C18_before_after`: Chapter 18C listings from 31 March 2023, beside the Base Rate and Southbound turnover.
